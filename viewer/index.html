A karmic map of the Earth

Prototype. Each hexagon carries the summed score of every recorded soul whose suffering fell inside it, for the chosen years. Colour is log10 of the score.

Sources in this build: UCDP GED 25.1 (one-sided and civilian deaths, 1989–2024); Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade Database 2019 (embarkation, Middle Passage, bondage); Wikidata camps with recorded death counts; Seguin–Rigby lynching supplement 1883–1941; a curated table of bioarchaeological sites from 800 kya onward (forensic evidence, genus Homo, combat assemblages excluded); Colonial Frontier Massacres in Australia 1788–1930 (Ryan et al., University of Newcastle, 438 sites); a draft curated table for Aotearoa New Zealand (Musket Wars, New Zealand Wars, Moriori, Parihaka). Polygon and route footprints are drawn at H3 resolution 4 (about 1,770 km² per cell); point sites at resolution 7 when zoomed in. Souls are counted per episode, so one enslaved person appears in up to three episodes.
